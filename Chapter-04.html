4.2 Semantic Layout Elements (Report Structure)

Semantic layout tags help search engines and accessibility tools understand the purpose of different parts of the educational portal.

<header>: Contains the main heading and EduHub logo.

<nav>: Contains primary links like Dashboard, Courses, and Settings.

<main>: Contains the unique content of this page (e.g., the full study material).

Mathematics Module Report

<section>: A standalone piece of content, such as a major sub-topic. This section covers algebra practice.

Learning Strategy

<article>: A self-contained piece of content, like a published article or blog post. This article details the 2026 study plan.

<aside>: Content related to the main content but separate from it, like a **Study Tips

<footer>: Contains copyright information, educational links, and <address> data (see below).
