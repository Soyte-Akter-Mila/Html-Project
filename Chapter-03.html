3.1 Formatting Elements (Document Emphasis)

Formatting elements are used to give text specific meaning or visual emphasis, critical for clarity in educational notes and study guides.

3.1.1 Bold (`<b>`) - Visual Emphasis

Used to draw attention without implying strong importance.

Key Formula: The Pythagorean theorem states that a² + b² = c².

3.1.2 Italic (<i>) - Alternate Voice

Used for technical terms, foreign phrases, or thoughts.

The Latin phrase Q.E.D. means "which was to be demonstrated."

3.1.3 Underline (<u>) or Insert (<ins>)

<u> renders a simple underline. <ins> indicates text that has been added to a document (useful for updated notes).

Geometry Module terms apply.
Updated Policy: Extra practice sheets provided for students.

3.1.4 Strikethrough (`<s>`) or Delete (`<del>`)

Used to show data that is no longer accurate or has been removed.

Old Formula: E = mc³
Incorrect Definition (Revised in updated notes)

3.1.5 Highlight (`<mark>`) - Key Information

Highlights text for reference or importance.

Please note that the Exam Date is fixed and cannot be changed.

3.1.6 Subscript (`<sub>`) and Superscript (`<sup>`)

Essential for mathematical formulas and footnotes.

Water formula: H2O
Area of a square: a2
